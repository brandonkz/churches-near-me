New Presbyterian, Anglican, Catholic and Community Church Listings: 5 June 2026

Updated 5 June 2026 - latest verified listings added from the local ChurchesNearMe directory

The 5 June ChurchesNearMe update adds seven verified church listings across Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal and the Western Cape. This batch is especially useful for people searching around Johannesburg's Parkview and Braamfontein areas, Durban's Glenwood and Pinetown areas, and Cape Town's Kirstenhof and Mowbray areas.

That suburb-level coverage matters. Most people do not choose a church from a national list alone. They start with a practical search like "church near me", then narrow the options by travel time, denomination, family needs and whether the church has enough current contact detail to confirm the next service.

This update strengthens the Johannesburg, Durban and Cape Town church directories while adding fresh pages for Presbyterian, Anglican, Catholic, community and non-denominational searches.

New Johannesburg Listings

Johannesburg gets three new listings in this update: two in Parkview and one in Braamfontein. That is useful coverage because both areas serve distinct search patterns. Parkview is a residential suburb where visitors often compare nearby family-friendly options, while Braamfontein is closer to the city centre, universities and weekday commuter routes.

  • St Columba's Presbyterian Church Parkview - a Presbyterian listing on Lurgan Road in Parkview.
  • St Francis of Assisi Anglican Church Parkview - an Anglican listing on Tyrone Avenue in Parkview.
  • Holy Trinity Catholic Church Braamfontein - a Catholic listing on Stiemens Street in Braamfontein.

For someone searching in Parkview, having Presbyterian and Anglican options in the same local area makes comparison easier. For someone searching near Braamfontein, a direct Catholic listing helps avoid results that only point back to broader Johannesburg pages.

These pages also connect naturally to the denomination hubs for Presbyterian churches, Anglican churches and Catholic churches. That gives visitors two clean routes into the same information: by place or by tradition.

New Durban Listings

Durban gains two listings in this batch, one in Glenwood and one in the Pinetown area. Both are practical additions because Durban searches often split quickly by suburb. Someone in Glenwood may not want to travel north or west on Sunday morning, while someone in Pinetown may prefer a nearby church over a central Durban result.

  • Glenwood Community Church - a community church listing on Bulwer Road in Glenwood.
  • His Church Pinetown - a non-denominational listing in Cowies Hill Park, Pinetown.

These listings are useful for visitors who are less focused on a traditional denomination and more focused on worship style, community fit, youth or children's ministry, and whether the church is close enough to visit consistently.

If you are starting from a wider search, the Durban church directory is still the best hub. From there, you can compare central Durban, Glenwood, Pinetown, Westville, Umhlanga and other local options without losing the bigger picture.

New Cape Town Listings

Cape Town gets two Anglican listings in this update: one in Kirstenhof and one in Mowbray. These are different search areas, but both are useful for people looking for Anglican churches close to home rather than only browsing the wider metro.

  • Church of the Holy Spirit Kirstenhof - an Anglican listing on Raapkraal Road in Kirstenhof.
  • St Peter's Church Mowbray - an Anglican listing on Church Street in Mowbray.

Kirstenhof is useful for southern suburbs searches around Tokai, Bergvliet, Retreat and surrounding areas. Mowbray helps people searching closer to the university corridor, Observatory, Rosebank, Rondebosch and the main transport routes into town.

Both pages strengthen the broader Cape Town church directory and the Anglican churches page. For visitors, that means the same church can be found from a city search, a denomination search or a direct suburb-style search.

How to Use This Update

If you are looking for a church this weekend, use this update as a shortlist rather than a final answer. Open the closest listing, compare one or two nearby options, and then confirm the current details directly with the church before you visit.

  • Check the latest Sunday service time before arriving.
  • Ask about children's ministry if you are visiting with family.
  • Confirm parking, public transport access and accessibility if the area is unfamiliar.
  • Look for current notices around school holidays, public holidays and special services.
  • Visit more than once before deciding whether a church is a good long-term fit.

ChurchesNearMe is built to make that first comparison easier. It helps you find nearby options, understand the basic location and denomination, and know what to confirm before you walk in on a Sunday.
