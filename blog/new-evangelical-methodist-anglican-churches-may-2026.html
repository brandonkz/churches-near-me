How to Use These New Listings

A verified listing is a good starting point, but it is still worth checking the details before you arrive. Service times can change around school holidays, public holidays, combined services, confirmations, funerals and seasonal events.

  • Start close enough to repeat. A church that fits your weekly route is easier to visit consistently.
  • Compare the denomination honestly. Anglican, Methodist, Presbyterian, Evangelical, Charismatic and non-denominational churches can differ in liturgy, preaching style, music and governance.
  • Check practical needs first. Ask about parking, children's ministry, youth groups, accessibility, language, small groups and newcomer follow-up.
  • Visit more than once. One Sunday can be unusual. Two or three visits usually gives a better read on the community.
  • Look for a next step. A clear welcome process, small group path or pastoral contact makes it easier to move from visiting to belonging.
