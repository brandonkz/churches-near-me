New Anglican, Methodist and Catholic Church Listings: 28 May 2026

Updated 28 May 2026 - latest verified listings added from the local ChurchesNearMe directory

Three of the new listings are in Johannesburg, with Rosebank, Orchards and Morningside now better represented. The remaining additions strengthen the Durban and Cape Town directories through Pinetown and Constantia.

How to Use This Update

A directory update is a starting point, not the final decision. The goal is to make it easier to build a realistic shortlist before you visit.

  • Search by suburb first. A church that fits your normal route is easier to visit consistently.
  • Check denomination fit. Anglican, Methodist and Catholic churches can feel quite different in worship style and weekly rhythm.
  • Confirm service details. Times, holiday schedules, parking and children's ministry details can change.
  • Visit more than once. One Sunday gives a first impression. A few visits gives a better read on the community.
