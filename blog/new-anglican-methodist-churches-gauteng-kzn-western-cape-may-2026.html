New Anglican and Methodist Church Listings: 25 May 2026

Updated 25 May 2026 - latest verified listings added from the local ChurchesNearMe directory

The 25 May ChurchesNearMe update adds nine verified church listings across Gauteng, KwaZulu-Natal and the Western Cape. This batch is especially useful for people comparing Anglican and Methodist churches by suburb, because the new listings sit in practical local search areas rather than only broad city centres.

Most church searches are local before they are theological. Someone in Discovery, Weltevreden Park, Pinetown or Kenilworth is usually trying to work out what is close enough to visit this Sunday, whether the tradition feels familiar, and where to check the latest service details. These new pages make that first comparison easier.

How to Use This Update

A verified directory listing should help you build a shortlist, not replace a real visit. Before you arrive, check the church's own website or social page for current service times, public holiday changes, parking notes, children's ministry information and accessibility details.

  • Start with the suburb. A church that fits your weekly route is easier to visit consistently.
  • Compare the tradition. Anglican and Methodist churches can differ in liturgy, music, communion practice, preaching style and governance.
  • Check practical fit. Parking, kids ministry, youth groups, language, livestreams and small groups may shape the decision as much as location.
  • Visit more than once. One Sunday can be unusual. Two or three visits usually gives a clearer read on the community.
